# Interior Design — Content Plan

Central teaching idea (every page reinforces it): **each method answers a different
question** — scan = what exists · plan = where things are · 3D = how it connects ·
render = what it looks like · video = what it feels like · presentation = why approve or
buy. Conclusion everywhere: combine the methods, do not choose one.

## Method pages — spec sections 20-26
Each page: eyebrow METHOD NN / question answered / what it is / what source material is
needed / what it shows / honest limits / best-for / best-combined-with / CTA.
Honesty lines kept: scans are a design and communication foundation, not engineering,
permit or survey certification; video is not measurement documentation; architecture stays
locked; plans remain the spatial source of truth.
